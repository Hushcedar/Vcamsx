Features

Virtual camera

Use selected videos or images in supported target apps, with in-app previews.

Picture controls

Rotate, mirror, zoom and reposition your picture from the floating panel.

Playback controls

Pause, resume, reload and choose 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× or 3× playback.

Live streaming

Connect HLS, HTTP, RTMP and RTSP media sources.

Audio injection

Select separate audio for supported voice notes or use video audio during supported calls.

Local monitoring

Hear injected audio while the microphone is active. Requires working device echo cancellation.

App cloning

Prepare, install and launch supported clones. Add them to your home screen and refresh their app source.

Target settings

Save media and injection settings for your supported target apps.

Seven themes

Choose VCAMSX Cyan, Verdant, Ember, Prism, Midnight Dark, Sapphire or Amethyst.

Compatibility depends on your phone, selected engine and target app version. Generated Chrome and Edge clones retain their original microphone audio.

Engine options

Host Clone

Prepare a separate APK for a supported target app. Keep the original app installed and approve the new installation when Android prompts.

Legacy · LSPosed

Enable the VCAMSX module, select your target apps in LSPosed and follow the in-app setup tutorial.

MochiCloner

Follow the supported no-root tutorial and enable VCAMSX for your clone in the helper’s module settings. Engine-selection text is available in English, French and Spanish.

Installation

  1. Download the APK. Open it on your Android phone and allow installation from your browser or file manager if prompted.
  2. Install or update. Install over your existing official VCAMSX app to keep its data.
  3. Choose your engine. Follow the in-app video tutorial and grant the permissions your selected features need, including overlay permission for floating controls.
  4. Select a target and media. Enable the matching injection setting, then open your prepared target app.
